# Systers Community

Welcome to Systers! 💜

Systers is a community for women in electrical engineering and computer science (EECS) to explore technology, share ideas, learn from one another, and build things together.

You do not need to be an experienced programmer to participate. Whether you're working on your first coding project, exploring a new research interest, or just curious about what other people are building, there's a place for you here.

## What can you do here?

- 🌱 **Learn:** Practice GitHub, explore new technologies, and share what you're learning.
- 💡 **Share ideas:** Tell us about projects you'd love to build, even if you haven't started them yet.
- 🛠️ **Collaborate:** Contribute to small projects and learn from other members.
- 📚 **Share resources:** Recommend tutorials, tools, articles, and other helpful resources.
- 🤝 **Connect:** Discover shared interests and find potential collaborators.

## How to get involved

1. Browse the repository and see what other members are interested in.
2. Add a project idea, resource, or introduction to the community board.
3. Make your first contribution using GitHub!
4. If you have an idea for a project, invite others to help you develop it.

New to GitHub? Start with [`WORKSHOP.md`](WORKSHOP.md) for our beginner-friendly tutorial.

Not sure what to contribute? That's okay! You can start with a question, an idea, or a resource you found useful.

## Our community values

- **Curiosity over experience:** You don't have to know everything to participate.
- **Collaboration over competition:** We learn more when we help one another.
- **Respect and inclusion:** Be kind, constructive, and welcoming.
- **Learning by doing:** Small contributions count!

## Start here

Check out [`community/PROJECT_IDEAS.md`](community/PROJECT_IDEAS.md) to share something you're interested in, or read [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) to learn how to contribute.

We're excited to see what we build together! 💜
